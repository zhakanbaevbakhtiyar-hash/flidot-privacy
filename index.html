Flidot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

Introduction

Flidot is a mobile puzzle game developed by Bakhtiyar Zhakanbaev. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect (spoiler: almost none) and how we handle it.

Data We Collect

Flidot does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or registration required
  • No personal information collected (name, email, phone, etc.)
  • No location data collected
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs

Local Storage

Flidot stores your game progress (completed levels, star ratings, and settings like volume preferences) locally on your device using standard system storage (UserDefaults on iOS). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Advertising

Flidot displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA with your permission)
  • IP address
  • General interaction data with ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Tracking on iOS).

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, Flidot will ask for your permission before allowing ad networks to track your activity across other apps and websites. You can change this at any time in your device settings. If you deny tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.

Children's Privacy

Flidot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The ads shown are standard Google AdMob ads with appropriate content filtering enabled.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements (Google Privacy Policy)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
